About Mayra

Mayra started as a small collection put together by someone who never found a piece that felt both delicate and durable enough to wear every day — most gold-tone jewellery is either too fragile to shower in or too heavy to layer. So we built the collection we couldn't find.

Everything here is 18k gold-plated stainless steel. That's a deliberate material choice, not a cost-cutting one: stainless steel doesn't tarnish or turn skin green the way brass or plain alloy does, and the plating holds up far longer than gold-plated brass — even with daily wear, sweat, and the occasional forgotten shower.

Care

  • Remove before showering, swimming, or exercising
  • Keep away from perfume, lotion, and chlorine
  • Store dry, in the pouch it arrives in, away from direct sunlight

Delivery

We deliver nationwide within Pakistan. Major cities: 2–4 working days. Other areas: 4–7 working days. Free delivery on orders over Rs 5,000 — otherwise a flat Rs 250 applies at checkout.

Returns & exchanges

Unworn pieces in original packaging can be exchanged within 7 days of delivery. Since earrings can't be resold for hygiene reasons, those are final sale unless faulty. Reach out on WhatsApp and we'll sort it out directly.
